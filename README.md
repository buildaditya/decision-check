# Decision Check

A lightweight personal decision journal.

Log a decision, write what you expect to happen, and rate your confidence.  
Later, record what actually occurred and see how accurate your judgment has been.

### What it does
- Quick decision logging
- Scheduled reviews (7 / 30 / 90 days)
- Simple accuracy tracking
- Local-only storage (your data stays in your browser)
- Export to JSON

### What it doesn’t do
No accounts. No cloud. No AI. No complex organization features.

### How to use
1. Open `index.html`
2. Log a decision
3. Come back when it’s due for review
4. Check your insights over time

---

Built to improve judgment through feedback, not to manage your entire life.
